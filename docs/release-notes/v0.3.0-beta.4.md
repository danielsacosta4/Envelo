Bank imports no longer inflate Ready to Assign.

Update with `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host).

## Already imported and Ready to Assign looks too high?

Go to **Accounts → Check accounts**. Envelo looks for:

- **History counted twice.** If an account was created with today's balance and its older history was then imported, that balance already included the history. The fix moves the starting balance to before the first imported row and lowers it, so today's balance stays the same.
- **Transfers counted as income.** Money out of one of your accounts and the same amount into another within 4 days. Linked as a transfer, it is no longer income in one account and spending in the other.
- **Duplicate "To categorise" envelopes**, left by earlier imports, merged into one.

Untick anything that isn't right, then press **Fix**. It can be undone.

## The import now shows what it will do

Before you import, a summary shows:

- how much goes to Ready to Assign, and from how many payments;
- how much goes to "To categorise";
- transfers between your accounts it recognised (rows that mirror one in another account are set to "↔ Transfer" automatically);
- a warning, with a fix ticked by default, when rows overlap the account's starting balance;
- when the bank file has a balance column: whether Envelo's balance will match the bank's afterwards.

Each row can also be set to **Don't import**.
