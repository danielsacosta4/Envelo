Delete many transactions at once.

Update with `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host).

## New

- **Delete transactions** on an account's page removes all of its transactions at once, or only the ones imported from a file, optionally between two dates. The starting balance is kept unless you untick it.
- Before deleting, it shows how many go, how the balance changes and which transfers also lose their other half in another account.
- It can be undone straight after with Undo or Ctrl+Z.
