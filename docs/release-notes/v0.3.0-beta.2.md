The redesign release. Envelo gets a bold new look, its own brand and sounds, plus the everyday tools that were missing: undo, split transactions, moving money, search, bank CSV import for any bank, receipts, a monthly recap and two-step sign-in. Shared envelopes can now receive money from the people you share them with.

![The new budget screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/danielsacosta4/Envelo/main/docs/screenshots/budget.png)

Update an existing install by running `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host). Your data is kept, and a backup is taken before every update.

## A new look

- **Poster design** on every page: big type, thick outlines and paper colours. Choose the Poster, Classic or Modern font in Settings.
- **16 group colours.** Pick one per group from the colour dot on the group header.
- **A celebration when every euro has a job**, with a stamp on Ready to Assign.
- **Sounds** made from paper, stamps, coins, a marimba and a till bell. They are off by default; turn them on in Settings.
- **The Envelo logo**, an app icon and a dark mode to match.

## Everyday budgeting

- **Undo** anything with Ctrl+Z or the button on the message that appears.
- **Move money** between envelopes, and cover an overspent envelope in one click.
- **Split transactions** across several envelopes.
- **Payee memory**: a payee fills in the envelope it was used for last time.
- **Search** across every transaction.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: press `?` to see them.
- **Receipt photos** on transactions, shrunk to WebP and stored encrypted.

## Bank import for any bank

Import a CSV export from any bank. Envelo works out the separator, date format, decimal comma and columns (Revolut and N26 are recognised), remembers the layout per account, skips duplicates and fills in envelopes from what each payee was used for.

## Recap, streaks and a setup guide

- **Monthly recap**: what came in and went out, what you kept, what was overspent, envelopes that keep running short, and where the money went.
- **Streaks** for months in a row with no overspending, with every euro assigned, and with money saved.
- **A setup guide** for new budgets.

## Sharing: others can add money

People you share envelopes with on "See them, add spending and add money" can now also add money. It goes into the account you choose and straight into the envelope, without changing your Ready to Assign. You see who added it, and they can delete what they added. Assigning money from your Ready to Assign is still only yours.

## Security

- **Two-step sign-in** with an authenticator app, plus recovery codes.
- **Change your password** in Settings. Other devices are signed out.
- **Delete your account** and everything in it.
- **Encryption at rest**: the budget file and receipts are encrypted on disk with a key kept in `/etc/envelo`.
- **Admin commands** in the container: `envelo users`, `envelo reset-password`, `envelo reset-2fa` and `envelo delete-user`.

## Installing

- The Proxmox installer has an Advanced menu, including which version to install.
- A new installer for any Debian or Ubuntu machine: `scripts/install.sh`.
- `update` keeps the last 5 backups.

## Known limitations

- The "In / out" column on the Shared page nets money added against spending.
- Offline mode only works over HTTPS.
