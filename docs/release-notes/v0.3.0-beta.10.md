Envelope targets on the Goals page.

Update with `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host).

## New

- Envelopes with a **Have a balance** or **Have a balance by a date** target now show on the Goals page, under "From envelope targets": progress, milestones, what to put aside each month to make the date, a forecast and a chart. **Edit** opens the envelope.
- Spending targets (monthly, weekly, refill, repeating bills) stay on the budget screen.
- Hidden envelopes are left out, and an envelope that already has its own goal is not shown twice.
- The goal card on the budget screen and the monthly recap include them too.
