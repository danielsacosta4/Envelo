Sub-envelopes, reordering, future transactions and PDF attachments, plus fixes for photos, the group picker and stickers.

Update with `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host). Your data is kept.

## New

- **Reorder groups and envelopes.** Press **Reorder** above your envelopes and move groups and envelopes up or down. Moving an envelope to another group puts it at the end of that group.
- **Sub-envelopes.** Put an envelope inside another, either from **Inside another envelope** when creating it or with **+ Add a sub-envelope** in the parent's settings. Sub-envelopes keep their own money, sit indented under the parent, and the parent shows how much is available across all of them. One level deep.
- **Future transactions.** Give a new transaction a date after today and it is scheduled: it waits under **Coming up** (budget page, next 30 days) and on the Accounts page, and only touches balances and envelopes on its day. Click one to change it, or × to cancel it. Income, spending and transfers can be scheduled.
- **PDF attachments.** Attach a PDF (up to 10 MB) as well as a photo to a transaction. PDFs are stored encrypted like photos and open in a new tab.
- **+ on each group** adds an envelope straight into that group.

## Fixes

- **Adding photos.** The photo button now works on phones that ignored it. Photos the browser cannot open (such as HEIC) are uploaded as they are or get a clear message. Large photos are made smaller so they pass reverse proxies with a 1 MB limit. If a proxy still refuses an upload, the message says so and how to fix it.
- **Group picker.** The group field in the envelope dialogs is now a list that always shows every group, with **+ New group…** to create one.
- **Stickers.** The envelope's sticker now has its own picker next to the name, so the phone's emoji keyboard is no longer needed.
- A photo added to a new repeating transaction was dropped; it now goes on the first one.
