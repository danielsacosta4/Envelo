Month fields that work in every browser.

Update with `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host).

## Fixes

- Month fields (a target's "By" date, "Until", a repeating bill's "Next due", a goal's date and a loan's first payment) were plain text boxes in Firefox, where typing "12" gave "Pick the month you want to reach the target". They are now a month list and a year list in every browser. Picking only a month fills in the year: this year, or next year if the month has already passed.
