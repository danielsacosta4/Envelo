Ready to Assign shows only money you really have after a bank import.

Update with `update` inside the container (or `pct exec <id> -- update` from the Proxmox host).

## Fixes

- **Spending in "To categorise" comes off Ready to Assign straight away.** Imported spending without an envelope used to count as overspending, which only comes off Ready to Assign the following month, so the current month showed spent money as money to assign. The "To categorise" row now says how much is waiting and links to those transactions.
- **Revolut exports in any language.** Exports in Portuguese, Spanish, French and other languages are recognised like English ones: reverted, declined and pending payments are skipped, fees are counted, and the completion date is used.
- When other envelopes are overspent this month, Ready to Assign says so and offers **Cover it now**.
