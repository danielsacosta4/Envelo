Envelo: five design directions

Each mockup shows the same budget (October, EUR) in a different style. Pick one or mix them. Numbers are sample data.

1. Envelope cards

Each envelope is a physical card that fills up. Easy to scan on a phone and makes overspending obvious at a glance.

Instead of a wall of numbers, the month ends with a short list of decisions. Each item explains what happened and offers one fix.

5. Plain-language nudges

The app talks to you like a helpful friend, not a spreadsheet. Each warning comes with a one-tap fix. The tone stays calm, and red is reserved for real problems.
